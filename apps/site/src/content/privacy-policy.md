# **Privacy Policy**

**Last updated:** March 27, 2026

AdLuv (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

AdLuv currently operates as a waitlist page and is available to users globally.

## **1\. Information We Collect**

We collect the following information:

### **a. Information you provide**

* Your email address when you join the waitlist

### **b. Website usage data**

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website.

This may collect limited technical information such as:

* Browser type  
* Device type  
* Pages visited  
* General location (e.g. city or country level)

We do not collect sensitive personal data.

## **2\. How We Use Your Information**

We use your information to:

* Manage the waitlist  
* Notify you about product updates and launch  
* Improve the website and user experience  
* Monitor and analyze usage of the site

## **3\. How We Share Your Data**

We do not sell your personal data.

We only share your data with trusted service providers necessary to operate the waitlist, including:

* Google Analytics (for analytics)  
* Brevo (for managing emails and communications)

These providers process data on our behalf and are required to protect it.

These providers may operate in different countries, and your data may be processed outside your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to ensure your data is protected.

## **4\. Legal Basis for Processing**

Under applicable data protection laws (including UK GDPR), we rely on:

* **Consent** \- when you provide your email  
* **Legitimate interests** \- to improve and operate the website

## **5\. Data Retention**

We retain your data:

* Until you unsubscribe or request deletion  
* Or as required by law

## **6\. Your Rights**

You have the right to:

* Access your personal data  
* Request correction or deletion  
* Object to or restrict processing

To exercise your rights, contact us at support@adluv.co

## **7\. Cookies**

We use cookies for basic functionality and analytics.

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## **8\. Data Security**

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

## **9\. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

## **10\. Contact**
